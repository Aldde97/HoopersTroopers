1. Who is responsible

Full name, street and number, postcode and city, Germany. Email: contact email. See also the legal notice.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your account: your email address, or the email address and account ID Google shares when you choose “Continue with Google”; the time you confirmed that you are 16 or older and accepted the terms. We need this to give you an account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your saved careers: the game data of your careers (clubs, players, results, the manager name you choose), stored so you can continue on any device (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Technical data: when you use the site, our hosting and account providers process your IP address, browser details and the time of requests in server logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a working and secure service).

Game analytics: We use PostHog in the EU to measure game opens, new careers, and matches played or simulated. Each page load gets a random identifier kept only in memory. Analytics events do not include account details, manager names, or saved careers. PostHog processes connection details to receive events. The game does not use analytics cookies or record sessions.

We do not show ads, do not sell data and do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

3. Data stored in your browser

The game stores your settings and careers in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB), and the sign-in session so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary to provide the game you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent banner is needed for it.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): accounts and cloud saves. Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). A data processing agreement is in place.
  • PostHog: game analytics, hosted in the EU.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc.): hosting and content delivery of the website.
  • Email provider, e.g. Resend: sends the sign-in emails.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the sign-in software library to your browser.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”; Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU standard contractual clauses. check each provider

5. How long we keep data

Your account and cloud saves are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account and all cloud saves at any time in the game (Settings › Account › Delete account). Optional: accounts unused for 24 months are deleted after an email reminder. Server logs are deleted after number days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability (you can also export any career as a file in the game). You can withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your federal state. Write to contact email for any request.

7. Age

The game is for people aged 16 and over.
